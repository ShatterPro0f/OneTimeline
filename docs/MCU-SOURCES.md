# Marvel Cinematic Universe — sources and how they are used

This document accounts for **every kind of data** in the Marvel Cinematic Universe pack: where it was found, how it was obtained, its licence or terms position, and what the app does with it. It follows the standard set by [ONE-CHICAGO-SOURCES.md](ONE-CHICAGO-SOURCES.md) and [CSI-SOURCES.md](CSI-SOURCES.md), per the rule in [CREATING-A-UNIVERSE.md](CREATING-A-UNIVERSE.md): no sources document, no place in the release.

**The pack in numbers:** 555 entries — 38 films · 489 series episodes · 3 Special Presentations · 8 one-shots (the 5 Marvel One-Shots plus the 3 Team Thor shorts) · 17 web entries (WHIH Newsfront 10, Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.: Slingshot 6, Stark Expo Videos 1) — with 554 descriptions and 544 runtimes. Streaming links are filled in progressively (§6): at the stage-1 build, 17 entries carry a link (4 Prime Video channel IDs, 12 Disney+ IDs, 1 Netflix ID) and the remaining 507 Disney+ IDs are collected by a separate, verified harvest before the pack is published.

**Form of the pack (Decision 1J):** this universe ships as a **downloadable pack**, not as data embedded in the app. The dataset file (`episodes-mcu.json`) is therefore self-describing: its envelope carries `displayName`, `showsLine`, and the `tvMazeShowIds` refresh mapping alongside `universe` and `version`, so a downloaded pack registers from its envelope alone.

The data was assembled by two build scripts, included in this repository under `tools/` so the pack can be rebuilt and audited: `build_mcu_episodes.py` (order snapshot + TVmaze + MCU Wiki) and `fetch_mcu_ids.py` (JustWatch Canada + the banked platform IDs). A full research report on this pack's sources was written before the build (`mcu-research.md` in the maintainer's records); the findings that matter are repeated here.

---

## 1. Watch order — the StorytellingDB timeline snapshot

- **Source:** [StorytellingDB](https://storytellingdb.com)'s MCU timeline table — an episode-level, in-universe-chronology ordering of the whole franchise, which uses Marvel's official timeline as its backbone and refines it to individual episodes and scenes.
- **How it was obtained:** the page renders its table (Ninja Tables) from a public data endpoint named in the page's own configuration; the complete table was retrieved from that endpoint on **2026-11-04** and the raw snapshot (637 rows) is retained in the maintainer's records. No account or permission was involved.
- **What was taken:** the **sequence itself** — the pack's watch order is the snapshot's order, entry for entry — plus each row's title and date for matching purposes.
- **The scope arithmetic (Sam's rulings, decision log §1F/§1H.1 — live action only):** of the 637 rows, **35 are tie-in comics** (not screen content), **44 are animation** (*What If…?* 26, *I Am Groot* 10, *Eyes of Wakanda* 4, *Marvel Zombies* 4 — animation is the one exclusion Sam ruled, because none of it is directly connected to the main MCU timeline), **10 are *Helstrom*** (excluded on a canon finding: its own showrunner stated the series is not tied to the MCU; this is the one judgement-call seam in the scope and can be reversed by the maintainer's word), and **1 is *Avengers: Doomsday*** (unreleased at snapshot time — it opens 2026-12-18 — so it has no metadata or links to carry yet). That leaves 547 rows.
- **Two expansions, both documented in §5:** the snapshot lists *Slingshot* as **one lumped row**; the pack expands it to its **6 episodes** (+5) at that slot. The snapshot does not list the **Team Thor** shorts at all; the pack adds all **3** at their story placements. 547 + 5 + 3 = **555 entries**.
- **Licence/terms position:** the ordering and entry facts are used as factual data; StorytellingDB is credited here and in the app (Settings → About).
- **How the app uses it:** the order is the tracker's sequence and the Up Next list; every other data kind hangs off it.

## 2. Order cross-check — the Disney+ official timeline row

- **Source:** Disney+'s own "Marvel Cinematic Universe Timeline Order" row, as transcribed on Wikipedia (the transcription was retrieved as page wikitext on 2026-11-04 and the 81 season-level blocks extracted verbatim).
- **How it was used:** as a **cross-check only** — it never overrode the snapshot (§1). Of the 81 blocks, **74 have in-scope pack entries**; the 7 without are exactly the excluded animation blocks (*Eyes of Wakanda*, *I Am Groot* seasons 1–2, *What If…?* seasons 1–3, *Marvel Zombies*).
- **Result: zero inversions.** No block the official row places earlier appears entirely after a later block in the pack. **Eight overlaps** exist, all of one kind — granularity, not disagreement: the official row files *She-Hulk: Attorney at Law* as a single block after *Echo*, while the episode-level snapshot spreads its nine episodes across their in-universe span (overlapping the *Eternals* / *No Way Home* / *Multiverse of Madness* / *Hawkeye* / *Moon Knight* / *Wakanda Forever* / *Echo* positions), and it places *Spider-Man: Far From Home* inside *The Falcon and the Winter Soldier*'s span, where its story sits. The pack follows the episode-level order throughout, per the accepted snapshot.
- **Not covered by the official row** (it is a Disney+ catalogue row, not a complete franchise record): *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.*, *Inhumans*, *Runaways*, *Cloak & Dagger*, *Slingshot*, *WHIH Newsfront*, *Team Thor*, *Stark Expo Videos*, the *Agent Carter* One-Shot, and the not-yet-streaming *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*. Their placements rest on the snapshot alone.

## 3. Episode identity, descriptions, and air dates — TVmaze

- **Source:** the [TVmaze](https://www.tvmaze.com) API (`api.tvmaze.com`), 26 series: Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. `31`, Agent Carter `336`, Inhumans `22928`, Daredevil `1369`, Jessica Jones `1370`, Luke Cage `2174`, Iron Fist `2175`, The Defenders `2176`, The Punisher `16544`, Runaways `20172`, Cloak & Dagger `15307`, Slingshot `23460`, WandaVision `41748`, The Falcon and the Winter Soldier `41749`, Loki `41007`, Hawkeye `43102`, Moon Knight `43518`, Ms. Marvel `43519`, She-Hulk: Attorney at Law `43517`, Secret Invasion `52352`, Echo `58950`, Agatha All Along `58948`, Ironheart `47859`, Daredevil: Born Again `63234`, Wonder Man `62569`, VisionQuest `64950`. (The same mapping travels inside the pack's envelope as `tvMazeShowIds`, so a downloaded pack can refresh itself.)
- **What was taken:** for all **495 series episodes** (the 489 episode-category entries plus Slingshot's 6): **season and episode numbers, canonical title, description, and air date**. The build verifies the join in both directions: the snapshot's parsed episode identities and TVmaze's (season, number) sets are identical for every series.
- **Coverage:** descriptions **494 of 495**. The one gap is *VisionQuest* 1x08 — the series is mid-run and its finale (airing 2026-11-10) has no published summary yet; the entry is otherwise complete.
- **Titles:** where the order source and TVmaze differ, TVmaze's title is used. All 40 such cases are punctuation-level (capitalisation, "S.O.S.: Part 1" vs "S.O.S., Part 1", the Jessica Jones season-3 "A.K.A" styling, *Orientation (Part One)* vs *Orientation, Part 1*) and each is logged by the build script.
- **Licence:** TVmaze data is **CC BY-SA**. The app credits TVmaze under every description it renders and in Settings → About, as the licence requires.
- **Deliberately NOT taken:** TVmaze's per-episode runtimes — for these series they are almost entirely absent, and where present they are slot lengths, not content lengths. Runtimes come from §4 instead.

## 4. Film metadata, true runtimes, and the Sony links — JustWatch (Canada)

- **Source:** [JustWatch](https://www.justwatch.com)'s public web API (the same unauthenticated GraphQL endpoint its website uses), queried for **Canada**, by `fetch_mcu_ids.py`. Nodes were discovered by title search and matched on title **and release year**; JustWatch's branding variants are normalised at match time ("Marvel One-Shot: Item 47", "Marvel's Jessica Jones", "The Fantastic 4: First Steps").
- **What was taken:**
  - **Films, Special Presentations, One-Shots — synopsis and runtime for all 49.** 46 synopses are JustWatch's; the 3 Team Thor shorts take theirs from the MCU Wiki (§5), as JustWatch carries none for them.
  - **True streamed runtimes for all 495 series episodes**, from JustWatch's episode records. A runtime was applied **only when JustWatch's episode title matched the pack's title** for that (season, episode) — a strict full-title comparison tolerant of punctuation variants only. The guard matters: JustWatch hosts same-title strangers (*The Defenders* 1961, *Runaways* 2006), and year-plus-coverage checks in the pipeline caught and rejected them. The final pass applied 495 of 495 with **zero unresolved mismatches**.
  - **The platform map** (which service streams what in Canada) behind §6 and §7: 542 of the franchise's 585 maximal-catalogue entries stream on Disney+ CA; nothing streams on base Prime Video; exactly one title streams on Netflix CA (*Spider-Man: Far From Home*).
- **The Sony Spider-Man films — Prime Video channel links.** *Spider-Man: Homecoming*, *Far From Home*, and *No Way Home* are Sony pictures; in Canada they stream through Amazon Channels, and their FLATRATE channel listings carry genuine Prime Video content IDs (`amzn1.dv.gti.…`). Those three IDs are in the pack as `prime` links: Homecoming and Far From Home via the **Starz Amazon Channel**, No Way Home via the **Crave Amazon Channel**. What that means for a viewer: the Play button opens the film in Prime Video as usual, but watching requires that channel's subscription on top of Prime. Purchase listings were never used as a source of IDs. *Far From Home* additionally carries its Netflix link (§6).
- **One more channel link, disclosed as a judgement call:** the same FLATRATE rule surfaced a **Crave Amazon Channel** ID for *Deadpool & Wolverine* — not a Sony film, but the identical species of link (a real Prime Video content ID for a film streamable inside Prime Video with the Crave channel). It is included and flagged here rather than silently dropped or silently kept.
- **Channel-link caveat, stated plainly** (the same position as the CSI pack's Paramount+ links): that an Amazon-Channel GTI deep-links through the app's Prime-on-Roku path exactly like a base-Prime ID is **inferred from the identical ID format, not yet device-tested** for these four films.
- **Licence/terms position, stated plainly:** JustWatch's published terms describe personal, non-commercial use. This project is free and non-commercial; the IDs are the platforms' own content identifiers (they name a title page; they are not video content), and no JustWatch prose beyond the short film synopses, artwork, or pricing data is used. The maintainer's decision to include the matched data, with the sources named here, is recorded in the project's decision log.

## 5. The web extras and shorts — MCU Wiki

- **Source:** the [Marvel Cinematic Universe Wiki](https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com) (Fandom), **CC BY-SA**, retrieved as page wikitext on 2026-11-04.
- **WHIH Newsfront — the canonical segment list.** The order source lists 10 WHIH rows; the Wiki's series pages confirm the canonical set is **exactly those 10** — season 1 (2015, the *Ant-Man* campaign): "WHIH Newsfront Promo", "WHIH Newsfront Top Stories", "WHIH EXCLUSIVE: 2012 VistaCorp break-in security footage involving cyber-criminal Scott Lang", "WIRED Insider Interviews Darren Cross, CEO of Pym Technologies", "WHIH EXCLUSIVE: Scott Lang Interview"; season 2 (2016, the *Civil War* campaign): "AVENGERS IMPACT: A WHIH Newsfront Special Report", "WHIH Newsfront: The Cost of Saving the World", "WHIH Newsfront: The Avengers and The White House", "WHIH Newsfront Exclusive: President Ellis Discusses the Avengers", "WHIH Breaking News: Attack in Lagos". The pack's WHIH entries carry the Wiki's segment titles, dates, and one-line synopses, seated at the snapshot's slots. One date dispute, decided for the Wiki: segment 1x03 is dated 2015-07-11 by the Wiki and 2015-07-10 by the snapshot; the pack uses **2015-07-11**.
- **Team Thor — synopses and placements.** Absent from the order source (§1), the three shorts are placed by their own story windows: *Team Thor* (2016, 3 min) and *Team Thor: Part 2* (2017, 4 min) immediately after *Captain America: Civil War* — they are Thor's account of sitting that conflict out — and *Team Darryl* (2018, 6 min) immediately after *Thor: Ragnarok*, whose ending it continues. Synopses and runtimes are the Wiki's.
- **Stark Expo Videos.** The snapshot's single 2010 row stands as **one lumped web entry**: the Wiki documents the Stark Expo 2010 promotional campaign as an in-universe website with videos (a welcome message from Tony Stark, a nostalgic 1974 Expo promotion), not an enumerable series, so there is no episode list to expand. Its description is written from the Wiki's account.
- **Slingshot cross-check.** The 6-episode expansion in §1 follows TVmaze's episode list (§3); the Wiki's series pages agree on the six titles and the 2016-12-13 release.
- **One-Shot naming:** the *Agent Carter* One-Shot (2013) shares its name with the series' first episode slot, so its pack entry carries the show name **"Agent Carter (One-Shot)"** to keep every entry's key unique. The other One-Shots stand under their own titles.

## 6. Streaming links — Disney+ and Netflix

- **The Disney+ ID model (proven on the maintainer's TV before this pack was built):** Disney+ deep links work with **entity/play UUIDs in bare form** — a series' entity UUID from its share link (`disneyplus.com/browse/entity-<uuid>`), and per-episode UUIDs from each episode's `/play/<uuid>` address. The `entity-`-prefixed form and Disney+'s older slug/video IDs do **not** work (they land on a "content not available" screen); both facts were established by on-TV tests, including two episode-exact opens.
- **The harvest method:** signed in to the maintainer's own Disney+ account, open each series page, take the series entity from the page address, open the EPISODES tab and read each episode tile's `/play/<uuid>` href **without playing anything** — metadata only, at browsing pace, under the maintainer's blanket scraping permission for his own accounts. IDs are never invented or derived; an entry without a harvested ID simply has no Disney+ link.
- **Banked at stage 1 — Loki, complete:** all **12 episode IDs** for *Loki* seasons 1–2 were harvested by this method before the pack build. The season-2 episode 4 ID is byte-identical to the one in a share link from the maintainer's own Disney+ app, and that ID was verified on his TV to open exactly that episode — the method and the data agree end to end. The remaining **507 entries** (70 titles: every film, special, one-shot, and series except *Loki*, *Runaways*, and the Sony films) are enumerated in the maintainer's harvest list and are filled by the same method before the pack is published. Four listed groups are flagged there as probably unfillable — *WHIH Newsfront* and *Stark Expo Videos* (YouTube/promotional web content, not Disney+ titles), *Slingshot* (no Canadian listing found at research time), and *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* (still in its theatrical window) — so the harvest can confirm and close them out rather than hunt for IDs that may not exist.
- **Netflix — exactly one title:** *Spider-Man: Far From Home* is the only MCU entry on Netflix Canada. Its link is the Netflix **title ID `81055822`**, banked from the maintainer's own Netflix (source URL retained in the maintainer's records) and **independently cross-checked**: the Netflix offer URL in JustWatch's Canadian data carries the same ID. (For films, a title ID is the complete link — Netflix film pages are title-level. Episode-level Netflix IDs are a different harvest and no MCU series needs one.)
- **Runaways carries no links at all:** in Canada it is purchase-only and does not stream on Disney+ CA, so its 33 episodes are checklist-only in the pack.
- **No crossover or tie-in flags** (decision log §1H.3): the pack ships **zero** event groups and zero cameo annotations, deliberately — in this universe nearly everything is connected, and the maintainer ruled the flags would carry no information.

## 7. Known gaps and the refresh caveat

- **Descriptions 554/555, runtimes 544/555.** The missing description is *VisionQuest* 1x08 (§3). The 11 missing runtimes are the *WHIH Newsfront* segments and *Stark Expo Videos*: no source publishes lengths for them (the segments run roughly one to three minutes), and the pack leaves the field empty rather than estimate.
- **Refresh caveat, disclosed for the app side:** the app's in-app TVmaze refresh validates a refreshed dataset before adopting it, and one of its rules requires every entry's runtime to be present and between 5 and 600 minutes. This pack cannot pass that rule — 11 entries have no runtime, and *Team Thor* Parts 1–2 run 3–4 minutes — so a refresh attempt for this universe will keep the shipped dataset (the designed fallback). The mapping itself is correct and travels in the envelope; pack updates are made by re-running the `tools/` pipeline, not by in-app refresh, until the app's validation grows a per-pack allowance.
- ***Avengers: Doomsday*** joins the pack only after its 2026-12-18 release gives it metadata and links to carry (§1).
