# Setup

## Install

1. Download the APK from the [Releases](https://github.com/ShatterPro0f/OneTimeline/releases) page.
2. Open it on your phone. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser or Files app — allow it for this install.
3. Open **One Timeline**. It installs as its own app and keeps its own progress.

## First run

The setup flow is skippable at every step, and everything in it can be changed later in Settings.

1. **Choose your universes** — the app starts empty: pick the universe packs you watch and download them (each is a one-time download from this repository). This step can be skipped entirely — packs can be added any time later in Settings, under **Get more universes**. Right below it, the **Universes** list chooses which of your downloaded packs to include; each keeps its own separate progress, and the same list lives in Settings.
2. **Starting point** — if you've already watched some of the universe, pick where you are and everything before it is marked watched. Nothing is pre-marked for you.
3. **Connect your TV (optional)** — tap **Find My TV** to discover a Roku TV on your home network, or enter its local IP address by hand and tap **Check connection**.
   - The TV and the phone must be on the **same network**.
   - On the TV: *Settings → System → Advanced system settings → Control by mobile apps* must be **Enabled**.

## Playing an episode

- The tracker's main button reflects the TV's real state: **Watch Now**, **Pause**, or **Continue Watching**.
- The app opens episodes in **Prime Video** on the TV. If Prime Video asks which profile to use, pick yours once — the app handles it from there on later plays.
- Episodes started from the app **resume** where you left them.

## Background watching

With **Background Watching** on in Settings, the app runs a small always-on notification while Prime Video is playing, so it can notice an episode ending and start the next one in the timeline — including switching shows for a crossover.

- Android may throttle background apps. For reliable switching, set the app's battery usage to **Unrestricted** (*Settings → Apps → One Timeline → Battery*). The Settings screen has a shortcut to the right page.
- You can pause, skip, or stop from the notification at any time.

## Troubleshooting

- **TV not found** — check both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, and that *Control by mobile apps* is enabled on the TV. A guest network or a router with client isolation will block discovery; entering the TV's IP address manually still works in that case if the network allows the connection.
- **Play opens the wrong episode page** — the episode's link can be corrected by hand: open the episode, and under **Your Prime Video link**, paste the right link from the Prime Video app (Share → Copy link). Your link always wins over the built-in one.
- **Background switching stopped** — re-check the battery setting above; some manufacturers' battery savers stop background services until the app is opened again.
